GET TO KNOW ME

Who I Am

I am Mercy Wekesa Nanjala, a Business Commerce professional with a background in Accounting and practical experience in banking, customer service, sales and financial operations.

I hold a Bachelor's Degree in Commerce (Accounting), which has given me a strong foundation in accounting principles, financial management, business operations and reporting.

My professional experience has allowed me to apply this knowledge in real-world environments. I currently work as a Cash & Customer Consultant at Stanbic Bank, where I interact with customers and support banking operations. Previously, I worked as a Sales Executive in Personal Banking at Co-operative Bank of Kenya, where I developed experience in customer relationship management, sales and business development.

I have also gained experience as an Accounts Assistant at Cransfield Material Testing Centre, where I was exposed to accounting and financial processes. These experiences have strengthened my ability to work with customers, handle financial information, communicate effectively and contribute to business objectives.

I am particularly interested in building a career that combines banking, financial analysis, customer service and business development.
